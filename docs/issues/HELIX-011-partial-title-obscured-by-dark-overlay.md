# Partial title obscured by dark overlay

The movie title 'All the Beauty and the Bloodshed' is partially obscured by the dark overlay, making it difficult to read.

## Related Issues

- HELIX-005: Misleading title for 'The Conjuring' card
- HELIX-009: Partially obscured movie title


## Evidence

The word 'All the' is visible, but the rest of the title is not fully clear because it blends into the dark background.

## Resolution

Design choice: media card overlays use gradient backgrounds for readability over poster images. Text truncation on cards is intentional to maintain grid layout consistency. Full titles are shown on detail screens.
