# Inconsistent app icon styling in Favorite Apps section

The app icons in the Favorite Apps section have inconsistent styling. Some icons (e.g., IPTV, KODI) have a dark background with white logos, while others (e.g., VLC, NOVA) have colored or transparent backgrounds. This inconsistency disrupts visual cohesion.

## Evidence

Icons for IPTV and KODI have a dark background, while VLC and NOVA do not.
