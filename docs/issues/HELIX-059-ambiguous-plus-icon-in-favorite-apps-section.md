# Ambiguous plus icon in Favorite Apps section

The plus icon in the Favorite Apps section is unclear in its purpose. Users may not understand if it is for adding a new app, a folder, or another function.

## Evidence

A plus icon without any label or clear context at the end of the Favorite Apps row.
