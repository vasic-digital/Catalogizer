# Low contrast between text and background

The gray text 'Media not found' on a black background may not meet accessibility standards for contrast, making it difficult for users with low vision to read.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the text readability in low-light conditions or for users with visual impairments.

## Evidence

Gray text on a black background has insufficient contrast.
