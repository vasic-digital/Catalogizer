# Inconsistent app icon styling in Favorite Apps section

The app icons in the Favorite Apps section have inconsistent styling. Some icons (e.g., VLC, KODI) have a distinct background and shadow, while others (e.g., IPTV, RUTUBE) appear flat and lack visual hierarchy.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the Favorite Apps row in the screenshot.

## Evidence

IPTV and RUTUBE icons appear flat without shadows or backgrounds, while VLC, NOVA, and KODI have shadows and distinct backgrounds.
