# Low contrast between text and background

The gray text 'Media not found' on a black background does not meet minimum contrast requirements, making it difficult to read for users with low vision or in bright lighting conditions.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the text readability under various lighting conditions or use a contrast checker tool.

## Evidence

Gray text on black background with insufficient contrast ratio.
