# No 'Forgot Password' or account recovery option

The login screen lacks a 'Forgot Password' or account recovery link, which is a common feature in login forms. This could frustrate users who need to recover their credentials.

## Evidence

No visible 'Forgot Password?' or similar link near the login fields.
