# Inconsistent app icon styling in Favorite Apps section

The app icons in the Favorite Apps section have inconsistent styling. Some icons (like VLC and NOVA) have vibrant colors and distinct shapes, while others (like KODI and Catalogizer) appear monochromatic or less visually engaging. This inconsistency can make the UI look unpolished and reduce visual cohesion.

## Evidence

VLC and NOVA icons are colorful and distinct, while KODI and Catalogizer icons are plain or monochromatic.
