# Unclear toggle status

The toggle switches for 'Recommended,' 'Subscriptions,' and 'Trending' could be confusing to users as the visual feedback does not clearly show what is being toggled on or off without prior experience.

## Evidence

The toggle states are indicated by a subtle color difference, but there is no additional indication (e.g., text or icon) to clarify their states.
