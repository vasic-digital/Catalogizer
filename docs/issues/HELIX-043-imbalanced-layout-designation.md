# Imbalanced layout designation

The interface appears to have an imbalanced layout with more visual weight on the left side of the screen, which could lead to confusion or misinterpretation by users. This can also be seen as a lack of harmony in design elements.

## Reproduction Steps

Navigate through screenshots of various mobile and desktop applications to observe the placement and distribution of content within each element of the interface.

## Evidence

The overall layout, text alignment, and use of whitespace are not consistent on both sides of the screen.
