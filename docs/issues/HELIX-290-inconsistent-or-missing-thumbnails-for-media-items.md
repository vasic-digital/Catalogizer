# Inconsistent or missing thumbnails for media items

The thumbnails for the recently added items appear to be generic or placeholder icons rather than actual previews of the content, reducing visual appeal and usability.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the thumbnails in the 'Recently Added' section.

## Evidence

All thumbnails show the same generic green icon with a play button, rather than unique previews.
