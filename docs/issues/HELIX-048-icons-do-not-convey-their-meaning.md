# Icons do not convey their meaning

The icons at the top of the screen do not clearly represent their intended function. This could cause users to misunderstand or overlook the actions available to them.

## Reproduction Steps

Looking at the icons and comparing them with standard iconography in applications of a similar nature.

## Evidence

The icons at the top right corner do not have clear labels or are visually overcrowded, making it difficult for users to understand their purpose.
