# Low contrast between text and background

The text 'Media not found' has low contrast against the dark background, which can make it difficult to read for users with visual impairments or in bright lighting conditions.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the error screen in various lighting conditions or with visual impairments.

## Evidence

Gray text on a black background reduces readability.
