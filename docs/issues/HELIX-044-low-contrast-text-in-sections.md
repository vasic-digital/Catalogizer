# Low contrast text in sections

The text 'Add and remove channels from your Home screen' and 'Send feedback' has low contrast against the dark background, making it difficult to read for users with visual impairments.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the text in the 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Send feedback' sections.

## Evidence

Gray text on a dark background reduces readability and fails WCAG contrast guidelines.
