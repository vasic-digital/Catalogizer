# Inconsistent text field styling

The text fields for 'Username' and 'Password' have a white background, while the 'Server URL' field has a dark gray background. This inconsistency can confuse users and disrupt the visual flow of the interface.

## Evidence

The 'Username' and 'Password' fields have a white background, while the 'Server URL' field is dark gray.
