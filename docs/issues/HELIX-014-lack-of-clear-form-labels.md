# Lack of clear form labels

The form fields do not have clear and descriptive labels, which can make it difficult for users to understand what information they should input.

## Related Issues

## Reproduction Steps

Look at the image and observe that there are no visible labels next to each field.

## Evidence

There are no labels or instructions indicating what the 'Username' and 'Password' fields are for, which can lead to confusion among users.
