# Lack of accessible contrast in text and background

The contrast between the gray text and the black or dark gray background is not sufficient, making it difficult to read for users with visual impairments.

## Evidence

The text 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Add and remove channels from your Home screen' is a light gray on a black background, which may not meet WCAG accessibility contrast standards.
