# Low contrast text on movie cards

The text on the movie cards (e.g., titles and ratings) has low contrast against the background, making it difficult to read for users with visual impairments or in bright lighting conditions.

## Evidence

White text on light or colorful backgrounds (e.g., '8-Bit Christmas') does not meet WCAG contrast guidelines.
