# Low contrast and small icon size reduce accessibility

The small, low-contrast icon in the center of the screen is difficult to see, especially for users with visual impairments. This violates accessibility guidelines for minimum contrast ratios and target sizes.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the icon in the center of the screen under normal lighting conditions.

## Evidence

Small, faint icon with insufficient contrast against the dark background.
