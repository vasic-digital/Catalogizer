# Insufficient color contrast for text and buttons

The text 'Unable to Load Media' and 'Media not found' as well as the button text may not meet WCAG contrast guidelines, making it difficult for users with low vision or color blindness to read the content.

## Evidence

Light gray text on a dark background may not provide sufficient contrast
