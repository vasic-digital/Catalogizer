# Missing accessible contrast ratio for text

The contrast ratio between the text color and the dark background may not meet WCAG guidelines, potentially making it difficult for users with visual impairments to read.

## Evidence

The placeholder text, labels like 'Server URL', and the button text are in gray shades that may not be sufficiently bright against the dark background.
