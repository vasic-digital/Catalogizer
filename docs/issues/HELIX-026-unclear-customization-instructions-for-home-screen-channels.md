# Unclear customization instructions for Home screen channels

The 'Customize your Home screen' section lacks clear interactive elements or visual cues on how to add or remove channels. Users may not understand how to perform the action described.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the 'Customize your Home screen' section and note the absence of buttons or interactive elements.

## Evidence

Text instructs users to 'Add and remove channels,' but no buttons, icons, or affordances are visible to guide the user.
