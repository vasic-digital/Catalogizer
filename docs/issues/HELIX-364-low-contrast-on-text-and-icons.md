# Low contrast on text and icons

The text and icons, particularly the play buttons and ratings, have low contrast against their backgrounds, making them difficult to read for users with visual impairments or in bright lighting conditions.

## Evidence

White text and icons on light or complex backgrounds (e.g., 'Fountain' thumbnail) reduce readability.
