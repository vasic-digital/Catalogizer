# Minimalist design may appear incomplete or broken

The extremely minimalist design with only a question mark icon may give the impression that the app is broken, incomplete, or still loading, rather than intentionally empty.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the empty state screen without prior knowledge of the app's design.

## Evidence

Lack of visual elements such as borders, illustrations, or loading indicators that suggest intentional design.
