# Low contrast between text and background

The text 'The Boss Baby - Family Business' and other UI elements have low contrast against their background, making it difficult for users with visual impairments to read.

## Evidence

Text 'The Boss Baby - Family Business' and rating '7.3' have low contrast on the dark gray background.
