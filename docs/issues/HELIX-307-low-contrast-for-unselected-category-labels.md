# Low contrast for unselected category labels

The text for the numerical counts and names of unselected categories (e.g., '10 Comics', '2 Software', '2 TV Shows', '1 Books') has insufficient contrast against the dark background. This makes the text difficult to read for many users, especially those with visual impairments, and fails accessibility guidelines.
