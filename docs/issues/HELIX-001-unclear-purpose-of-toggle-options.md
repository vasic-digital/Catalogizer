# Unclear purpose of toggle options

The purpose of the toggle options (Recommended, Subscriptions, and Trending) is not clear. It is not apparent what enabling or disabling these options will do for the user.

## Evidence

The options under 'YouTube channels' do not include any explanatory text or tooltips to clarify their functionality.
