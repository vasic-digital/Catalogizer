# Unclear purpose of the '+' icon in Customize section

The '+' icon next to the 'Customize your Home screen' text is ambiguous. Users may not understand if it is clickable or what action it performs without additional context or labels.

## Evidence

The '+' icon appears without a clear label or tooltip explaining its function.
