# Inconsistent app icon styling in Favorite Apps section

The app icons in the Favorite Apps section have inconsistent styling. Some icons (e.g., IPTV, KODI) have a solid background, while others (e.g., VLC, NOVA) do not, leading to a visually disjointed appearance.

## Evidence

IPTV and KODI icons have a distinct background color, while RUTUBE, VLC, and NOVA do not.
