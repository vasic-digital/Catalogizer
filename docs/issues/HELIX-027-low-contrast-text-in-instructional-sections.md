# Low contrast text in instructional sections

The text 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Add and remove channels from your Home screen' has low contrast against the dark background, making it difficult to read for users with visual impairments.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the text in the 'Customize your Home screen' section.

## Evidence

Gray text on a dark gray/black background reduces readability.
