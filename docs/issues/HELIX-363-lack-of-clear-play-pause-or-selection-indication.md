# Lack of clear play/pause or selection indication

The play button overlay on thumbnails is subtle and may not be immediately clear to all users, especially those unfamiliar with the interface. This could lead to confusion about how to interact with the content.

## Evidence

Small play button icons on thumbnails are faint and may not stand out against varied backgrounds.
