# Misaligned or inconsistent spacing in YouTube channels section

The spacing between the 'Recommended,' 'Subscriptions,' and 'Trending' items in the YouTube channels section is inconsistent, leading to a cluttered appearance.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the vertical spacing between items in the YouTube channels section.

## Evidence

The spacing between 'Recommended' and 'Subscriptions' is tighter than between 'Subscriptions' and 'Trending'.
