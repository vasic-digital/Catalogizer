# Inconsistent app icon styling in Favorite Apps section

The app icons in the Favorite Apps section have inconsistent styling. Some icons (e.g., IPTV, RUTUBE, KODI) have a distinct background and border radius, while others (e.g., VLC, NOVA) appear flat and lack borders.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the Favorite Apps section in the screenshot.

## Evidence

IPTV, RUTUBE, and KODI icons have a 3D-like appearance with shadows, while VLC and NOVA icons are flat and rectangular.
