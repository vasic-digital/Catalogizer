# Inconsistent width of action buttons at the bottom

The 'Back to Library' button is noticeably wider than the 'Play' and 'Favorite' buttons, leading to a less uniform and visually balanced appearance for the group of action buttons at the bottom of the screen. While text-driven, a more consistent width or adaptive design could improve overall polish.
