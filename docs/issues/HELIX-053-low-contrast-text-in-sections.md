# Low contrast text in sections

The text in the 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Send feedback' sections has low contrast against the dark background, making it difficult to read for users with visual impairments or in bright lighting conditions.

## Evidence

Gray text on dark background in 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Send feedback' sections.
