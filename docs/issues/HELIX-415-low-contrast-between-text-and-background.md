# Low contrast between text and background

The loading text is gray on a dark background, which may not meet WCAG contrast guidelines (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text). This reduces readability for users with low vision or in bright lighting conditions.

## Evidence

The text 'Loading your media collection...' appears in a light gray color on a dark gray/black background.
