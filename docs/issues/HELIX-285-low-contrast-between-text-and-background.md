# Low contrast between text and background

The text 'Media not found' and the 'Back' button have low contrast against the dark background, which may make it difficult for users with visual impairments to read.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the text and button in the error state.

## Evidence

The gray text and button on a black background do not meet WCAG contrast guidelines.
