# Low contrast loading indicator

The 'C' logo loading indicator has low contrast against the dark background, making it difficult to see for users with visual impairments or in bright lighting conditions.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the loading screen in various lighting conditions or use a contrast checker tool.

## Evidence

The faint blue 'C' logo on a dark background does not meet WCAG contrast ratio requirements.
