# Placeholder icons lack clarity in YouTube Channels section

The placeholder icons for 'Recommended', 'Subscriptions', and 'Trending' in the YouTube Channels section are generic and do not clearly represent their purpose. This may confuse users about the function of each option.

## Evidence

Placeholder icons next to 'Recommended', 'Subscriptions', and 'Trending' are indistinct circles with no clear meaning.
