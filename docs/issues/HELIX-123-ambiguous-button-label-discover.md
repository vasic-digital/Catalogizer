# Ambiguous button label 'Discover'

The 'Discover' button under 'SERVER CONNECTION' is ambiguous and does not clearly communicate its function. Users may not understand what it is meant to discover (e.g., servers, media, etc.).

## Evidence

The label 'Discover' is vague and does not specify what action it performs.
