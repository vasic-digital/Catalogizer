# Lack of feedback for inactive options

The toggles for 'Recommended' and 'Trending' are inactive and their visual appearance does not provide sufficient feedback to indicate that they are disabled.

## Evidence

Toggles for 'Recommended' and 'Trending' appear grayed out but do not indicate why they are disabled or how to enable them.
