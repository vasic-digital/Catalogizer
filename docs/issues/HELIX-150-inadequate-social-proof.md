# Inadequate social proof

The presence of social media account login options does not provide clear or convincing evidence that other users have successfully used these features, which can make potential users hesitant to attempt the same process. Inclusion of trust badges or testimonials would help build confidence in the system.

## Reproduction Steps

Users may be more likely to choose a different authentication method if they do not see strong evidence that other users have successfully used these social media account login options. They might also be concerned about privacy and security issues associated with third-party logins.

## Evidence

There are no visible trust badges or testimonials indicating that other users have successfully logged in using their social media accounts, which can create a sense of uncertainty for potential users.
