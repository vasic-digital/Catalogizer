# Lack of visual feedback for interactive elements

The 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Send feedback' sections lack clear visual indicators that they are interactive or clickable. Users may not realize these are actionable options.

## Evidence

No hover, underline, or button styling is present for 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Send feedback'.
