# Inconsistent button style

The 'Sign in' button has a different design than the other buttons on the page. The inconsistency in button styles can make it confusing for users to understand which elements are clickable and how they should interact with the interface.

## Reproduction Steps

Users may not immediately recognize that this is the primary call-to-action because of the differing design compared to other buttons on the page. They might be more likely to click on another button by mistake or become frustrated if they do not understand how to proceed.

## Evidence

The 'Sign in' button has a different color scheme and font weight than the other buttons, which creates visual inconsistency.
