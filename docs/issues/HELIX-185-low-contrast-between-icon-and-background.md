# Low contrast between icon and background

The small curved arrow icon has low contrast against the dark background, making it difficult to see for users with visual impairments or in low-light conditions.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the icon in various lighting conditions or use a contrast checker tool.

## Evidence

The icon color blends into the dark background, reducing visibility.
