# Prominent use of capital letters

The use of all caps for 'Customize your home screen' and 'Add channels from your social media' can be considered too aggressive, as it may cause visual fatigue or be perceived as overly forceful. It could also hinder the readability of the text.

## Related Issues

- HELIX-048: Icons do not convey their meaning
- HELIX-091: Icons not aligned within the menu


## Reproduction Steps

Click on the menu icon to open the menu

## Evidence

Screenshot shows that the options in the menu are presented entirely in uppercase letters.
