# Misplaced elements on the form

The 'Password' field should be placed above the 'Confirm Password' field, as it is common practice in form design. Additionally, the 'Name' field could benefit from being labeled clearly.

## Related Issues

- HELIX-005: Unclear call-to-action
- HELIX-008: Input field placeholder text is missing
- HELIX-009: Button alignment is inconsistent
- HELIX-011: Color contrast is insufficient


## Evidence

In the image, you can see that the 'Name' field is placed below the 'Password' field. This may lead to confusion and make it harder for users to enter their information in a logical order.
