# Lack of clear call-to-action

There is no clear CTA for the user. Users may not know where to click or what to do next.

## Reproduction Steps

Look at the screenshot and observe the lack of a prominent button or link to guide the user on what to do.

## Evidence

The image shows a 'Password' field, but there is no button or link for users to proceed with their login attempt. This may result in user confusion and frustration.
