# Generic and inconsistent button styles

The 'Sign In,' 'Discover,' and 'Connect' buttons have inconsistent visual hierarchy, making it difficult for users to quickly determine the most important action.

## Evidence

The buttons have a flat design with no distinction in color, size, or placement, leaving the primary action ambiguous.
