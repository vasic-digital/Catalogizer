# Inconsistent app icon styles in Favorite Apps section

The app icons in the Favorite Apps section have inconsistent styles and shapes. Some are square, some rounded, and some have no background, which disrupts visual uniformity and makes the UI look unpolished.

## Evidence

IPTV, RUTUBE, VLC, NOVA Video Player, and KODI icons vary in shape and style.
