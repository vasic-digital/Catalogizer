# Icons and Text Placement in Navigation Bar

The navigation bar has an unusual placement of icons and text, which may cause users to misunderstand the functionality or hierarchy of options. There is no consistent pattern in how items are arranged.

## Reproduction Steps

Navigate through the app to view the top navigation bar

## Evidence

Screenshot shows the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen, with text and icons placed in an irregular manner that may confuse users.
