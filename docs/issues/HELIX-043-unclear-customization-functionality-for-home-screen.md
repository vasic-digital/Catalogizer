# Unclear customization functionality for Home screen

The 'Customize your Home screen' section lacks clear interactive elements or instructions on how to add/remove channels. Users may not understand how to perform the action described.

## Reproduction Steps

Observe the 'Customize your Home screen' section and note the lack of buttons or interactive affordances.

## Evidence

The section only contains text and a non-interactive icon, which does not clearly indicate how to proceed.
