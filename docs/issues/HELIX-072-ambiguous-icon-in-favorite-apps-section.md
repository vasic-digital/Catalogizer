# Ambiguous '+' icon in Favorite Apps section

The '+' icon in the Favorite Apps section is unclear in its purpose. Users may not immediately understand if it is for adding a new app, a shortcut, or a widget. This ambiguity can lead to confusion and reduce ease of use.

## Evidence

The '+' icon lacks a label or tooltip explaining its function.
