# Inconsistent button labeling

The labels for buttons 'Sign in' and 'Create account' are inconsistent in terms of wording. 'Sign in' is commonly used while 'Create account' may be a bit vague or unclear. Consistency in labeling can improve user understanding and reduce confusion.
