# Insufficient color contrast on toggle switches

The light blue toggle switch color against the dark background does not meet WCAG guidelines for sufficient color contrast, making it difficult for users with visual impairments to perceive.

## Evidence

Toggle switches (e.g., for 'Subscriptions' and 'Trending') have a light blue color that blends with the dark background, reducing visibility.
