# Informational content within settings menu

The 'customize your home screen' notification does not provide sufficient context about the potential benefits of customizing the home screen, which may deter users from engaging with this feature.

## Reproduction Steps

View the notification and attempt to gather information about what customization entails.

## Evidence

Notice that the notification does not elaborate on the benefits or process of customizing the home screen.
