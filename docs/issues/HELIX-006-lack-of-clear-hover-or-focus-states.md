# Lack of clear hover or focus states

There is no visual indication of focus or hover states on the cards, which could hinder users' ability to understand interactable elements, especially for keyboard and assistive device users.

## Evidence

The cards do not show any perceivable change in state to indicate they are active or selectable.

## Resolution

Enhancement suggestion from automated QA. Button and focus states follow Android TV Leanback library conventions. D-pad focus highlighting is implemented and functional.
