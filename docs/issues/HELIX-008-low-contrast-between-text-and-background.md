# Low contrast between text and background

The text, such as 'Customize your Home screen,' has low contrast against the dark background, making it harder to read for users with visual impairments or in low-light environments.

## Evidence

Text on a dark gray background in the left-side panel (e.g., 'Customize your Home screen') is barely readable.
