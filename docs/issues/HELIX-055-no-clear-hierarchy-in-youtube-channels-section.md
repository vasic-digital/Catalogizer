# No clear hierarchy in YouTube Channels section

The YouTube Channels section lacks a clear visual hierarchy. The 'Recommended', 'Subscriptions', and 'Trending' options appear as equally weighted, which may not align with user priorities or typical usage patterns.

## Evidence

All three options in the YouTube Channels section are styled identically without emphasis.
