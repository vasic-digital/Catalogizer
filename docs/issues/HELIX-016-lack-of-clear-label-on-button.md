# Lack of clear label on button

The button in the image is labeled 'Sign up'. While it is common for such buttons to be associated with account creation, there is no visible text or icon that indicates this action will create a new account. This lack of clarity can cause confusion and may lead to users inadvertently signing into an existing account.

## Related Issues

- HELIX-013: Lack of clear call-to-action
- HELIX-014: Lack of clear form labels


## Reproduction Steps

Click on the 'Sign in' button.

## Evidence

Button with no clear indication of its purpose or functionality.
