# Low contrast and small font size for 'Favorite Apps' label.

The 'Favorite Apps' label in the top-left corner is rendered in a small font size and appears to be a dark grey against a black background. This combination results in low contrast, potentially making the text difficult to read for users with visual impairments or when viewing the screen from a typical TV viewing distance.

## Related Issues

- HELIX-005: Low contrast for descriptive text in cards.


## Evidence

The text 'Favorite Apps' in the top left corner, which is small and grey against the black background.
