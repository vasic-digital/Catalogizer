# Low contrast for descriptive text in cards.

The sub-description text ('Add and remove channels from your Home screen' and 'Have an issue or a suggestion?') in the two large cards has a dark gray color on a slightly lighter dark gray background. While readable for some, the contrast ratio might be low, potentially making it difficult for users with visual impairments or in certain viewing conditions to read the text.

## Evidence

The light gray text ('Add and remove channels...') against the dark gray background of the 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Send feedback' cards.
