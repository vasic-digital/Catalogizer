# The 'NOVA' app button appears visually truncated on its right edge.

The blue background of the 'NOVA' app button in the 'Favorite Apps' row seems to be slightly cut off or abruptly ended on its right side. It does not maintain the consistent rounded rectangle shape seen in other well-rendered app buttons like 'IPTV' or 'VLC'.
