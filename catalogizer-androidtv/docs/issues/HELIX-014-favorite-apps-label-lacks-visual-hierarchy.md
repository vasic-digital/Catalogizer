# 'Favorite Apps' label lacks visual hierarchy.

The 'Favorite Apps' section header text is quite small in font size, making it less visually prominent compared to other elements on the screen. A slightly larger font size or bolder weight would improve its readability and establish better visual hierarchy for the section, making it easier for users to identify the section.

## Related Issues

- HELIX-001: App icon partially obscured by adjacent icon in 'Favorite Apps' row.
- HELIX-002: Missing visual focus indicator for selected app.
- HELIX-009: Secondary text in information cards is difficult to read due to small size and blurriness.


## Reproduction Steps

Observe the 'Favorite Apps' label at the top left of the screenshot.

## Evidence

The font size of 'Favorite Apps' is noticeably smaller than the titles of the cards below ('Customize your Home screen', 'Send feedback'), making it blend in rather than stand out as a section header.
