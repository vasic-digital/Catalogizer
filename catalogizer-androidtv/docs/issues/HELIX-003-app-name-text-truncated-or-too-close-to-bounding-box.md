# App name text truncated or too close to bounding box.

The 'IPTV Pro' app name displayed below its icon appears to have its 'o' character slightly cut off or is positioned too close to the right edge of its designated display area. This affects the professional appearance and can slightly hinder readability.

## Evidence

The 'o' in 'Pro' under the 'IPTV' icon is very close to or slightly merges with the right edge of its text container.
