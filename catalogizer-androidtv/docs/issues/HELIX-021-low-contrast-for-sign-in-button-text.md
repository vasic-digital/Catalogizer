# Low contrast for 'Sign In' button text

The text 'Sign In' on the button has insufficient contrast against its dark grey background. This can make it difficult for users with visual impairments to read and identify the button, impacting accessibility and usability.

## Evidence

The 'Sign In' button, showing light grey text on a dark grey background.
