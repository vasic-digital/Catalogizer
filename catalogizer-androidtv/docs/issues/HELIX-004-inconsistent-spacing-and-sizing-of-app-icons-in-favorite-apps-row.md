# Inconsistent spacing and sizing of app icons in 'Favorite Apps' row.

The spacing between app icons (e.g., the gap between 'VLC' and 'NOVA' is very small, while other gaps are larger) and potentially their overall size/padding appears inconsistent. This creates a less organized and polished visual presentation for the 'Favorite Apps' section.

## Related Issues

- HELIX-003: App name text truncated or too close to bounding box.


## Evidence

Compare the narrow space between 'VLC' and 'NOVA' icons to the wider space between 'KODI' and 'NETFLIX'. Also, the 'IPTV Pro' block appears to occupy more horizontal space than 'VLC' or 'KODI', even accounting for the text below.
