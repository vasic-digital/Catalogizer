# Secondary text in information cards is difficult to read due to small size and blurriness.

The descriptive text in the 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Send feedback' cards ('Add and remove channels from your Home screen' and 'Have an issue or suggestion? Provide feedback about Android TV.') appears very small and slightly blurry, especially for a TV interface viewed from a typical distance. This can make the content difficult to read and impacts the overall user experience and accessibility.

## Related Issues

- HELIX-001: App icon partially obscured by adjacent icon in 'Favorite Apps' row.
- HELIX-002: Missing visual focus indicator for selected app.


## Evidence

The smaller, secondary text within the 'Customize your Home screen' and 'Send feedback' cards at the bottom of the screen.
