# Inconsistent sizing and padding for app buttons in 'Favorite Apps' row.

The app buttons in the 'Favorite Apps' row exhibit inconsistent widths and padding. For example, 'IPTV' and 'VLC' are wider, while 'NOVA', 'KODI', 'NETFLIX', and the '+' button are narrower. This leads to a visually disjointed experience and can make some app names appear cramped (e.g., 'NETFLIX' text nearly fills its entire button width).
